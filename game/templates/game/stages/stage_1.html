You have been dragged before the magistrates in the Roman agora! Stripped and beaten with rods, your feet are fastened in heavy stocks.

To crack the magistrate's lock, search Acts 16:16–24:

  1. Digit 1 How many apostles were seized and dragged into the marketplace?
    HintRead Acts 16:19–20.
  2. Digit 2 What is the single final digit of the verse in which the spirit left the girl at that very hour?
    HintLook at Acts 16:18.
  3. Digit 3 Calculate the magistrate's tally: garments torn off them + missionaries beaten with rods + inner prison doors locked behind them.
    HintRead Acts 16:22–24. Count each thing as it happens in those verses, then add the three counts together.
