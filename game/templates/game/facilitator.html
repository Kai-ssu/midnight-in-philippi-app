Running the Game

Everything for the night, in order. The full host script, answer key and hint guide are in your printed Facilitator Guide.

1 · Before players arrive

  1. On the projector laptop, open the backdrop and press F for full screen.
  2. Players scan the QR code on the backdrop, or type the address below. One phone per team works best.
  3. Make sure every team has an NIV or NKJV Bible. Teams choose which one in the app before they begin.
Open the backdrop Backdrop without timer

2 · Start

  1. Read the host script aloud.
  2. Press Space on the backdrop to start the room timer, and tell every team to tap Begin at the same moment.

3 · During the game

  1. When teams reach Stage 3, press E on the backdrop for the earthquake.
  2. Each clue has its own hint button, so step in only when a team has been stuck for a while.
  3. When a team escapes, open their Victory Box. Reset for a new team on their screen clears the phone for the next group.

4 · Debrief

  1. On the backdrop, move the mouse and choose Debrief (or press D).
  2. Use → and ←, or a presentation clicker, to move through the three questions.
